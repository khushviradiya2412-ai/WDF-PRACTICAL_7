Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Student Hub Portal

General Questions

The Student Hub Portal is a comprehensive digital platform designed to streamline academic and administrative processes for students. It provides a centralized location for course management, grade tracking, attendance monitoring, event registration, and communication with the institution.

To create an account, click on the 'Register' button on the login page. Fill in your personal information, set a strong password, and complete the verification process. You'll receive a confirmation email to activate your account.

Yes, the Student Hub Portal is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. You can access it on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Simply open your web browser and navigate to the portal URL.

The portal supports all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, please ensure your browser is updated to the latest version.

Academic Questions

Log in to your account and navigate to the Dashboard. You'll see your grades for all enrolled courses. You can also view detailed breakdowns of assignments, exams, and your current CGPA.

Go to the Course section in your Dashboard, select the specific course, and find the Assignments tab. Click on the assignment you want to submit and upload your file before the deadline. Remember to save your submission.

Yes, you can download your attendance report from the Attendance section in your Dashboard. The report can be exported as a PDF or Excel file with detailed daily records.

GPA is calculated based on course credits and grade points earned in each course. The system automatically computes your Semester GPA (SGPA) and Cumulative GPA (CGPA) according to the institutional grading policy.

Account & Security

Click on the 'Forgot Password?' link on the Login page. Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a password reset link to create a new credential.

Navigate to the Profile page from the top navigation menu. Click on 'Edit Profile' to modify your contact information, address, and profile photo.
